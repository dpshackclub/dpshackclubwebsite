// 01 · THE MANIFESTO

Writing code on paper is not coding.

Somewhere along the way, computer science became a memorisation subject. Underline the keyword. Draw the flowchart. Get 18/20. Never run the program.

Hack Club DPSN is the antidote. We open laptops, break things, explore ideas, and actually ship — sites, games, hardware, and creative experiments. Hack Club is a U.S. 501(c)(3) nonprofit educational charity whose mission is to help build a generation of teen makers. Explore its official programs. Curiosity mandatory.

  • NOSlide decks read out loud, word for word
  • NOPrior experience, fancy laptops, or gatekeeping
  • YESFree hardware, stickers, grants and pizza
  • YESShipping something ugly today > planning forever

// 02 · THE VIBE

What we actually do

(spoiler: not attendance)

01
👾

Build weeeeird stuff

Creative coding, web experiments, hardware builds, and workshop projects. Hack Club is about making cool things, learning by building, and sharing what you ship.

#creative-coding #ship-it
02
🍕

Free gear & YSWS grants

Hack Club's global "You Ship, We Ship" programs mail real hardware, domains and swag to students who build. Yes, to Nacharam. Yes, actually free.

#ysws #free-hardware
03
⚡

Zero lectures, ever

No syllabus, no unit test, no "this will come in the exam". Lofi on the speaker, laptops open, seniors helping juniors. That's the whole format.

#chill-space #peer-learning
